# Hr-Analytics-Dashboard

Developed an interactive HR MIS Dashboard in Excel to monitor employee performance, attendance trends, salary distribution, and attrition analysis across multiple departments. The dashboard helps HR teams and management track workforce metrics and support data-driven employee management decisions.

⸻
The HR department lacked a centralized reporting system to monitor employee performance, attendance, salary patterns, and attrition trends. Manual tracking made it difficult to identify workforce issues, department performance, and employee retention risks efficiently.
⸻

 Analysis Performed

* Analyzed employee distribution across departments including HR, Finance, IT, Operations, Marketing, and Sales
* Evaluated average salary and attendance trends department-wise to identify workforce performance patterns
* Performed employee segmentation using Age Groups and Salary Bands for better workforce understanding
* Conducted attrition analysis to identify employee turnover and workforce retention insights
* Compared experience levels with salary trends to understand compensation patterns across employees

⸻

Dashboard Features

* KPI Cards for Total Employees, Avg Salary, Avg Attendance, and Attrition Count
* Department-wise employee distribution analysis
* Salary and attendance performance tracking
* Interactive slicers for Job Role, City, and Work Mode filtering
* Age Group and Salary Band segmentation visuals
* Experience vs Salary trend analysis

⸻
 Insights Delivered

* Identified departments with the highest employee concentration and attrition count
* Highlighted attendance performance trends across different departments
* Enabled HR teams to monitor salary distribution and workforce segmentation effectively
* Improved workforce reporting efficiency through centralized and interactive MIS reporting
* Supported data-driven HR decision-making for employee management and retention planning

⸻

 Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers
* Data Cleaning & Formatting
* KPI Reporting
